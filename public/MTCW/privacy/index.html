Who is responsible.

[to be confirmed: legal controller identity] operates under the trading name MT Creative Works. You can contact us about personal information at [to be confirmed: verified privacy contact]. Our relevant business contact details are [to be confirmed: verified business disclosure].

What we collect.

When you send an enquiry through the contact form, we receive the details you enter. We collect them from you directly.

The form asks for:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your business area (Wirral, Liverpool, or both)
  • your message about what your business does and what you would like from the website

You can also choose to add:

  • your business name
  • what you are interested in (the website, monthly changes, both, or not sure yet)
  • a phone number
  • your town or district
  • an existing website or social media page
  • whether you would prefer a video call or an in-person planning meeting

If you become a client, we also keep the information needed for the agreed work and the correspondence that goes with it.

To limit spam and repeated sending, the form includes a hidden field that people do not normally see. The website also keeps short-lived counters of recent submissions, and a short-lived note that an enquiry has just been sent so that it is not delivered twice. These are filed under a one-way coded value made from your network (IP) address and the enquiry, so they do not contain your IP address, name, email address or message.

Why we use it.

We use enquiry information to reply to you, understand your requirements and prepare a proposal when you ask for one. Client information supports the work we agree to carry out. Necessary technical information helps us operate the website and protect it from misuse.

What you need to provide.

To deal with an enquiry we need your name, your email address, your business area and your message. Without them we cannot understand your enquiry or reply to it. Everything else on the form is optional.

The form does not ask for payment details, a budget, a file upload or an account, and you do not need to agree to marketing to send an enquiry.

Who receives it.

The website does not keep its own copy of your enquiry. When you send the form, it passes your details to an email delivery service, which delivers them to the mailbox where we receive enquiries. That service and our mailbox provider handle what you send as part of delivering it to us.
