Discuss changes

A subscription is a choice, not a default.

We do not automatically move you onto a monthly plan after the website is built or simply because you make an enquiry. When you choose a subscription, its agreed terms explain when it starts, how it is billed and how it can end.

  • Asking about a change

    Asking does not start billing. We explain the proposal and obtain your agreement before activating a chargeable changes service.

  • If something in our build is wrong

    Report it to us. Correcting an error in our implementation is different from requesting a new feature or a later content change; it does not automatically require a subscription.

  • Once you choose a subscription

    The proposal explains its duration, billing and cancellation or pause arrangements before you accept it. Do not assume that an active plan stops billing automatically in a month without changes.

What happens after the first year?

The £250 offer covers the first 12 months online. It does not include hosting and domain renewal forever. We explain the arrangements and costs for keeping your website online after that period before you place the initial order.

Continuing the website does not automatically mean joining a changes subscription.

What about something outside the agreed website?

Requirements such as an online shop, custom software, specialist booking tools, photography, business mailboxes or ongoing marketing need their own scope. We discuss any additional cost before you commit. They are not hidden compulsory extras for the agreed standard website.

Questions, answered.

Not answered here? Ask us.

Is the £250 a monthly price?

No. It is a one-off payment for the agreed initial website, domain and first 12 months online.

Do I have to take a subscription?

No. A changes subscription is optional. The first-year website offer does not depend on taking one.

What happens when I want a change?

Tell us what you need. We review the work and agree a monthly price and scope from £100 before you decide whether to proceed.

Does asking for a change automatically start billing?

No. We explain the proposal and obtain your agreement before activating a chargeable changes service.

Can I cancel or pause a subscription?

The proposal explains its duration, billing and cancellation or pause arrangements before you accept it. Do not assume that an active plan stops billing automatically in a month without changes.

Is the planning meeting included?

Yes. One initial one-hour meeting is included with the website project, by video or in person in Wirral or Liverpool.

Can I use an existing domain?

We can discuss it. We check who controls it, how it is currently used and what connection or renewal arrangements are needed before making changes.

What if I do not have the words or photos ready?

Bring what you have. We use the planning meeting to identify what the website needs and agree how the remaining content will be prepared.

Can I review the website before it goes live?

Yes. The agreed initial website is reviewed before publication. The scope and review process are explained as part of the project.

What if something in the original build is wrong?

Report it to us. Correcting an error in our implementation is different from requesting a new feature or a later content change; it does not automatically require a subscription.

How long will the build take?

We agree the timetable around the scope, availability and information needed. We do not promise a launch date before understanding the project.

Will the website bring in enquiries?

It can give people a clearer way to understand and contact your business. We do not guarantee enquiries, revenue or a particular Google ranking.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us about the business. We'll help you choose a sensible starting point.
