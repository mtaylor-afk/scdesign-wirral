About this website.

MTCreativeWorks.co.uk explains the services offered under the trading name MT Creative Works by [to be confirmed: legal supplier identity]. Our required business and contact details are [to be confirmed: verified business disclosure].

Enquiries and agreements.

Sending an enquiry does not create an order, activate a subscription or book a meeting. Before paid work starts, we agree a written proposal and service agreement covering the project and its practical terms.

The £250 first-year offer.

The £250 is a one-off payment, not a monthly charge. It covers the agreed initial website, domain and first 12 months online, including one initial one-hour planning meeting by video or in person in Wirral or Liverpool. The pages, content, features and relevant dates are agreed for the project.

No changes subscription is required to receive that included year.

Optional changes.

Monthly changes are optional and start from £100. They are additional to the £250 initial payment. The agreed price depends on the scope and complexity of the work. A proposal explains its duration, billing and cancellation arrangements before you accept it. A request for information or a change does not itself activate billing.

After the included year.

The £250 offer is not lifetime hosting or domain registration. Arrangements and costs beyond the first 12 months are explained before the initial order. They do not automatically enrol you in a changes plan.

Content, review and additional work.

We agree the content needed for the project and the review process. You approve your business details and confirm that materials you supply may be used. Requests outside the agreed scope are discussed before additional charges arise.

Correcting an error in our implementation of the agreed website is different from a new request or a later content change. It does not in itself require a changes subscription.

Ownership, access and handover.

The service agreement explains domain registration, rights in the delivered website, any third-party licences and the arrangements for access or handover. These details are agreed before work starts.

Using this website.

Please do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorised access or submit unlawful material. The site's branding and content belong to their respective rights holders. Showing a project does not grant rights to reuse its assets.

Links and examples.

External websites have their own terms. Project examples demonstrate different scopes and are not a promise that every displayed feature is included in the £250 offer. We do not guarantee a particular search ranking or commercial result.
