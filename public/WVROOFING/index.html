How a re-roof works

A clear process, start to finish

A typical semi-detached re-roof takes about three to five working days once the scaffold is up, weather permitting.

  1. Free survey

    We inspect the roof, timbers and chimney, and bring real samples of the tiles you liked in the visualiser.

  2. Written quote

    A fixed, itemised price and a clear plan. We'll also check whether your roof needs planning or Building Regulations paperwork.

  3. Scaffold & strip

    Scaffolding and protection go up, then the old coverings and battens come off. Any tired timbers are dealt with.

  4. Membrane & battens

    A breathable membrane and new treated battens go on, with ventilation designed in.

  5. Your new roof

    Tiles or slates, a dry ridge and verge, leadwork and flashings, finished neatly.

  6. Sign-off & tidy

    A final walk-round with you, the paperwork, and every scrap of waste taken away.

Questions

Before you re-roof

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most terraced and semi-detached houses take around three to five working days once the scaffold is up, weather permitting. Larger or more complex roofs take longer, and we'll give you a realistic timetable with your quote.

Do I need planning permission for a new roof?

Usually not for a like-for-like replacement in England. If your home is in a conservation area or is a listed building, or you'd like a noticeably different material, you may need consent. We'll check before any work starts.

Does a new roof need Building Regulations approval?

A full re-roof normally does. It counts as renovating a "thermal element", which can mean upgrading the roof insulation at the same time. We'll explain what applies to your roof and how the sign-off is handled.

How accurate is the Roof Visualiser?

It's a concept tool to help you choose. Quick previews are approximate, and photo-real renders are AI illustrations, not photographs of finished work. Screens show colour differently too, so we always bring real samples to your survey.

Can I stay at home while the work is done?

Yes. Roofing happens outside. We protect your garden, paths and windows, and leave the site tidy at the end of every day.
