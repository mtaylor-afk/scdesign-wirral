Privacy

Privacy notice

What happens to your photo in the Roof Visualiser, and to the details you send us.

Concept draft. WV Roofing is a concept and isn't trading yet. This notice describes how the site works today and must be reviewed, with the business's legal details added, before launch.

Who we are

WV Roofing is a proposed roofing business covering the Wirral and Liverpool. The data controller's name and address will be added here before launch. Until then, you can use the contact details in the contact section.

The Roof Visualiser and your photo

  • On your device first. When you add a photo, your browser shrinks it and removes hidden information such as GPS location. Marking the roof and the instant quick previews all happen on your own device. Nothing is uploaded at that stage.
  • Photo-real renders are optional. They're only created if you switch them on. Your photo and the roof outline you drew are then sent securely to our server, which forwards them to OpenAI (a US company acting as our processor) to generate the images.
  • Not stored by us. Our server handles the photo in memory and doesn't save it. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models by default, and it may keep requests for up to about 30 days for abuse monitoring, under its current terms.
  • A tip: photos from the pavement work best. Try to avoid people and readable car number plates.
  • The sample houses on the site are stock photographs. Number plates and house numbers are blurred.

Quote requests

If you send a quote request, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone or email, postcode (optional), your preferred roof and your message. If you choose to include your visualiser images, they're attached too. The request is emailed to the WV Roofing team and used only to reply to you and prepare a quote. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. (Retention period to be confirmed before launch.)

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Vercel runs the server functions for renders and quote requests.
  • OpenAI generates the photo-real renders, and only if you ask for them.
  • Apple iCloud Mail delivers quote-request emails.
  • Google Fonts serves the Inter typeface to devices that don't have Apple's system font, so your browser may connect to Google to download it.

There are no advertising or analytics cookies on this site, so there's no cookie banner. Your browser may remember small preferences locally.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it's used. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
