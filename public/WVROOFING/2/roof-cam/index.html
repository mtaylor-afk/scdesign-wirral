ConceptPreviews are approximate. Nothing is uploaded.

WV Roofing is a hobby concept and isn't trading. Every roof you see here is drawn on your own device from numbers, keeping your photo's light and shadow, and is approximate. The weather is a mood, not a forecast. Your photo is never uploaded or stored, and it only leaves your device if you share or save a picture yourself.
