ConceptPreviews are approximate. Your photo is kept privately.

WV Roofing is a hobby concept and isn't trading. Every roof you see here is drawn on your own device from numbers, keeping your photo's light and shadow, and is approximate. The weather is a mood, not a forecast. The photo you add is saved to WV Roofing's private store so the roofer can see it: it's kept for 30 days unless you send them an enquiry, and you can delete it while it's on screen, or ask the roofer later. Sample houses are never saved. What's kept, and for how long.

Photograph your house. Tap the roof. See eight roofs on it, in the weather. Then send the one you like to the roofer.

  • Stand across the road, so the whole roof is in view.
  • Daylight is best. Hold the phone level.
  • On a phone, Photograph my house opens the camera. A photo from your library works too.

Your photo is saved for the roofer. The roofs are drawn here, on your device. The photo you add is also kept in WV Roofing's private store (where saving is switched on: a light under the controls tells you), so the roofer can see it: for 30 days, or with your enquiry if you send one.
