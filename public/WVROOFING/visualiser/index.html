    Mark your roof.

    Tap round the edge of each roof slope, then tap the first point again to close the shape. Cut out chimneys and roof windows so they stay as they are.

    Roof marked0%

    Tips for marking
    • Each roof slope can be one shape. Semis and terraces: mark only your own half.
    • On a phone, press and slide to fine-tune a point with the magnifier, then lift your finger.
    • Double-click, press Enter, or use "Finish shape" to close a shape.
    • Cut out chimneys, roof windows and solar panels. The renders keep them as they are.
