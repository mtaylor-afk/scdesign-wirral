Roof replacement in Wirral & Liverpool

A new roof is a big decision and a long-term one. Here's what's involved, what's included, and how to choose the right tiles or slates for your home.

Is it time?

Signs your roof needs replacing

A few slipped tiles can usually be repaired. When problems keep coming back, a full replacement is often the better investment.

  • Slates sliding out of place. On older slate roofs the nails rust through ("nail fatigue"), so slates keep slipping however often they're refixed.
  • Damp patches or leaks in the loft or bedroom ceilings, especially after heavy rain.
  • A sagging ridge or dipping roof line, which can point to tired battens or timbers.
  • Crumbling felt or no underlay, visible from inside the loft.
  • Porous, flaking or heavily mossed tiles that hold water and crack in frost.
  • Age. Many concrete-tiled roofs across Merseyside are now 50 to 70 years old.

What's included

A complete re-roof, done properly

Every quote is itemised, but a typical WV Roofing replacement covers:

  • Scaffolding, edge protection, and covers for your garden and paths
  • Stripping the old tiles or slates, battens and felt
  • Checking rafters and replacing any damaged timber (priced separately if needed)
  • A breathable roofing membrane and new treated battens
  • Your chosen tiles or slates, fixed to current standards
  • A mechanically fixed dry ridge and dry verge (no mortar to crumble)
  • New leadwork and flashings around chimneys and abutments
  • Roof ventilation to help prevent condensation in the loft
  • Removing all waste, and a final walk-round with you

Regulations & paperwork

Planning, Building Regulations and your roof

Planning permission. Replacing a roof like-for-like doesn't usually need planning permission in England. Homes in conservation areas, listed buildings and properties covered by an Article 4 direction are different, and a noticeably different material can need consent. Wirral and Liverpool both have many conservation areas, so we check before any work starts.

Building Regulations. A full roof replacement normally needs Building Regulations sign-off, because it counts as renovating a "thermal element". That can mean upgrading the loft or rafter insulation at the same time. We'll explain what applies to your roof and how the sign-off is handled.

This is general guidance for England, not legal advice. Always confirm with your local authority if you're unsure.
