What SC Design Wirral does

SC Design Wirral helps homeowners turn home-improvement ideas into clear design drawings for planning, building regulations, lawful development certificates and builder quotations. Typical projects include rear, side, wraparound and kitchen-diner extensions; loft conversions and dormers; garage conversions; front porches; bespoke garden rooms; internal reconfiguration; conservation-sensitive design; change of use; concept and feasibility; and measured surveys.

Important wording rules

  • Acceptable descriptions: architectural designer, architectural design service, architectural design and drawing practice, Chartered Architectural Technologist, MCIAT, planning drawings, building-regulations drawings, residential design, builder-ready drawing packs.
  • Please do not describe the business as “architects”, “registered architects”, “RIBA architects” or “ARB-registered architects”. In the UK the title “architect” is protected by law and reserved for ARB-registered professionals. Sean Corser MCIAT is a Chartered Architectural Technologist; SC Design Wirral is an architectural design and drawing practice, not an ARB-registered architect practice unless that is separately confirmed.
  • Design only. Despite the registered company name containing “Construction”, the practice prepares designs and drawings and does not carry out the building work itself. Homeowners use the drawings to obtain like-for-like quotations from builders they appoint.
