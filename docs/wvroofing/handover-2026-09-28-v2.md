# WV Roofing: handover v2 (2026-09-28)

This hands over the v02 build at the end of **Release B's required increments (B1–B4)**. It replaces v1 for
current status; v1 (end of A7) is kept.

- The plan is `~/.claude/plans/dazzling-herding-starfish.md` (v02). Decisions are in `decisions.md`.
- Status uses the brief's words, with nothing rounded up:
  - **IMPLEMENTED:** code exists.
  - **CONFIGURED:** real credentials and settings are supplied.
  - **INTEGRATION-TESTED:** the actual provider was exercised successfully.
  - **VALIDATED:** the end-to-end result passed the relevant quality checks.
  - **PRODUCTION-READY:** the release's gates passed.

## Where things stand

- **A1** is **live** (verified 2026-09-28).
- **A2–A7 and B1–B4** are built and tested in the labelled test environment. They are committed on
  `feat/wvroofing` but **not pushed**: push gate D17 holds them until Neon and Vercel Blob are connected.
- **A8** (live integration with the real providers) waits for the owner's steps and his OK on spend.
- **B5**, the optional operator desk-measure tool, needs a separate approval and 20–30 reference
  properties. It isn't started.
- **Release A is built but not live.** A customer can go through the whole journey: their home, or photo
  only; upload, mark, compare, quick previews and photo-real renders; an enquiry stored before any email.
  The operator can open it, correct the scope, request a survey and delete it. All of this is tested
  locally only.
- **Release B's assisted route is built.**
  - The roofer enters, approves and labels measurements.
  - The customer sees figures only when they're approved and the source allows showing them, with the
    disclaimer and the list of what isn't included.
  - Quantities come only from **verified** products. All nine pre-filled product specifications are
    drafts, so today customers see the roof's size and no material quantities.
- **Public automatic measurement remains an unresolved requirement** (D11). No licensed provider allows
  showing derived figures to customers. The supplier enquiries in `supplier-enquiries.md` are drafted for
  Matthew to send.

## What the owner needs to do

These are in order, and in full in `setup-and-deploy.md`.

1. **Step 0: Vercel checks.** Note the plan, Fluid compute and the region; set Node 24.x.
2. **Step 1: storage.** A private Blob store `wvroofing` and a free Neon database `wvroofing`, both in
   London, connected.
3. **Step 2: core variables.** `WVR_SESSION_SECRET`, `CRON_SECRET`, `WVR_OPERATOR_PASSWORD_HASH`,
   `WVR_LEAD_TO` and `WVR_DAILY_BUDGET_USD`.
4. **Step 3: push the held commits** (Desktop **PUSH WV ROOFING.cmd**), then run the live checks.
5. **Step 4: switch on one capability at a time.** This includes `WVR_CAP_ASSISTED_MEASUREMENT=on` when
   you want customers to see measured roofs.
6. **Step 5: A8.** OK the render benchmark (about $3), pre-rendering the samples (about $2–3) and one real
   enquiry email.
7. **Release B inputs:**
   - the roofer checks each draft specification against its datasheet, and it's marked verified
     (`serverlib/wvroofing/products.json`);
   - their tolerances;
   - optionally, send the supplier enquiries.

## Flags

**Carried over from v1:**

- Hobby is non-commercial.
- Node 20 is deprecated on 1 October 2026.
- The region pin is deferred.
- SC's `sharp` needs upgrading.
- The home page's "Free … survey" wording is to be confirmed.
- `WVR_MAX_SEAM` and the gpt-image-2.5 prices are uncalibrated.
- The address-lookup cap is too high for the concept phase.
- Contact details can't be edited on the operator screen.
- SC's `CLAUDE.md` still holds an old credential.

**New in Release B:**

- **Draft specifications only.** Customers get no material quantities until the roofer verifies at least
  one product per look.
  - Slate figures assume **moderate exposure**; coastal Wirral may be severe, which needs more slates.
  - Two looks don't match their product's size exactly (noted on each draft).
- **No ridge figures.** No manufacturer publishes a ridge cover length, so ridges only get figures once the
  roofer adds a per-metre figure to a verified product.
- **Verifying a product is a file edit.** It's a change to `products.json`, not an operator-screen
  action.
